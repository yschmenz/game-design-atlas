---
title: First rooms that teach
by: schmenz
summary: Opening spaces that hand you a mechanic without ever saying "press X".
---

The best openings are silent tutorials — geometry, light and a single safe verb do the teaching.

- portal-series — the canonical case: the first chamber teaches the portal in three moves, no text.
- half-life-2 — the opening hands you the world (and your distrust of it) before it hands you a gun.
- the-witness — teaches you to *see* the line before it ever asks you to solve one.
- mirrors-edge — the first rooftop is one long lesson in reading the city as movement.
- bioshock — its opening teaches you to distrust the very first thing it tells you.
