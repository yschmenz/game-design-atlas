---
title: First rooms that teach
by: schmenz
summary: Opening spaces that hand you a mechanic without ever saying "press X".
games: [portal-series, half-life-2, the-witness, mirrors-edge, bioshock]
---

The best openings are silent tutorials — geometry, light and a single safe verb doing the teaching. Portal's first chamber and Half-Life 2's opening are the canon; the others each teach a different first lesson (seeing, moving, distrusting).
