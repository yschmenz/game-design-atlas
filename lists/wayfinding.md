---
title: Wayfinding without a map
by: jachym
summary: Getting the player where they need to go with light, landmarks and shape — no minimap.
---

A reference set for any jam where navigation is the problem.

- half-life-2 — framed sightlines and the canal-as-rail; the environment itself is the map.
- sable — landmarks on the horizon pull you across an open desert with no waypoints.
- outer-wilds — you navigate by curiosity and memory, never by markers.
- journey — a single beckoning mountain orients the whole game.
- mirrors-edge — runner vision paints the path directly into the world.
